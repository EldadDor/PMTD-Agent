# Later phases

G0 approved the roadmap arrangement on 2026-11-20. Phase 1C is active and
Phase 1D is next. The approved product plan remains the source for full scope.

- **1E — Notifications and feedback:** Depends on recommendation output, LINE
  quota and webhook verification, and feedback decisions.
- **1F — Operational hardening and MVP acceptance:** Depends on the end-to-end
  workflow and includes deployment, reliability, and unattended operation.
- **Phase 2 — Semantic retrieval:** Deferred until the MVP works and the user
  separately approves expansion.
